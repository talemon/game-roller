## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment (unanchored):** Authored, but the authorship lives in motion and color, not in structure. ~70% specific.

Specific: the five OKLCH facet hues threaded through chip fill/border/label/description and `accent-color` (FacetCard.svelte:262-270, 293) so a word traces to its compartment without a label; the rattle's dashed, blurred, fixed-width ghost chips on a `60 + 160·t²` tick curve (reveal.ts:83) reading as dice slowing rather than a spinner; `reservedRows` returning 0 for an untouched card (FacetCard.svelte:38); the dashed-border, collapsed-padding disarmed card.

Generic: **there is no tray.** A 960px column, a hero card, then five identical rounded rectangles at 0.75rem gap (App.svelte:154, 180-183). Strip the hues and the reveal and the geometry is a settings page. And the artifact isn't treated as an artifact — the sentence sits in the same `--surface`/12px/`--border` material as the theme toggle row. ## Overall Impression

The motion system is better than the product around it. The rattle, the hue traceability, the four independent escape hatches, the held no-lift discipline — that is real craft. But the product's single reason to exist (one sentence worth keeping) is the weakest-treated element on the page, and the reveal — its one authored moment — is broken at every slot boundary.

Biggest opportunity: **make the result an object.** Give the sentence its own material, make Copy the loud button once it exists, stop losing ideas on the next roll.

## What's Working

1. **The hue system is information design, not decoration.** `--facet-hue` (FacetCard.svelte:75) is one number per facet; lightness/chroma come from per-theme tokens (app.css:14-24 vs 56-67). Measured chip-label contrast 10.4–11.3:1 in both themes.
2. **The reveal is escapable four ways and one is permanent.** Skip button, Escape (App.svelte:99), unchecking mid-flight (App.svelte:48), persisted localStorage. Reduced motion swaps vocabulary — verified: no ghosts, 500ms/slot, no blanket `transition-duration: 0`.
3. **No-lift discipline is actually held.** `button:hover` changes only `border-color` (app.css:112-114); press is `scale(0.97)` at 0.05s; cards and chips are inert.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Skip flickers into an enabled Copy four times per reveal.**
`revealing` derives purely from `pendingFacetId` (App.svelte:27), nulled in `onLock` (App.svelte:73) and not re-set until the next `onSpin` after `GAP_MS = 220`. During each gap `{#if revealing}` is false, Skip unmounts, Copy mounts — enabled, over a half-built sentence. Measured 4 flips per 5-facet reveal, ~230ms each (t=1516/1747, 3231/3463, 4970/5175, 6684/6914 ms); reproduced independently by the detector agent in both motion modes (302ms and 182ms gaps).
*Why it matters:* for ~14% of the reveal, the thing under the cursor copies a truncated idea with no indication anything went wrong. Also drives the `role=status` churn.
*Fix:* make `skipReveal` a `$state` handle and `const revealing = $derived(skipReveal !== null)`, cleared only in `onDone`. Give the Skip/Copy slot a fixed `min-width`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The banner reflows mid-reveal and moves the Skip target under the cursor.**
`.sentence` reserves `min-height: 2.6em` (ResultBanner.svelte:122), `3.9em` under 640px. Measured 1440×900: banner 190px → 231px, button row y=256 → y=298 while the reveal was still running. At 390×844 a five-facet sentence rendered at 175px against an ~87px reserve.
*Why it matters:* the one place DESIGN.md explicitly promises reserved space over reflow, broken in exactly the configuration an engaged user reaches.
*Fix:* set `--lines` from `activeRolls.length`, or freeze the banner at post-roll height for the reveal's duration.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] On mobile, the sentence and the per-facet Roll buttons can never be on screen together.**
390×844, all five armed: banner bottom y=542; Roll buttons at y=646/846/1047/1294/1539; document 1800px, viewport 844. Nothing is sticky.
*Why it matters:* PRODUCT.md names per-facet re-roll as *the* core interaction. On a phone it becomes press → scroll up → read → scroll down → press.
*Fix:* `position: sticky; top: 0` on `.banner` below ~720px, or a sticky compact result bar carrying Copy.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] The sentence produces recognizable garbage often enough to break trust.**
(a) "game" twice — 7 of 127 genre items contain "Game" against the `A <prefixes> game about …` template (sentence.ts:39-42); ~11% of default 2-genre rolls, ~20% at 4. (b) `sampleDistinct` (roll.ts:3) guarantees distinct items, not distinct meanings; observed "…role-playing action role-playing game…". (c) `default: 2` genres (genre.ts:11) is the main source.
*Fix:* a `suppressesHead` flag for the seven "Game" tags; a family key on genre items rejected in `rollFacet` (roll.ts:23); reconsider `default: 1`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Three silent states teach the user that controls are advisory.**
(a) Every card unchecked → "Roll everything" enabled and inert (`play([])` → `onDone`, reveal.ts:87-89). (b) `99` in a count snaps to max on blur, unannounced. (c) Changing a count doesn't re-roll, so a card can read "How many 2" while showing 4 chips.
*Fix:* `disabled={armedCount === 0}` plus inline note; route the clamp through `role=status`; dim chips when `state.count !== state.rolled.length`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable harden`

**[P3] Copy is the success event and the weakest element on the page.**
Default-styled secondary next to an accent-filled primary; disabled until a sentence exists; unmounted for the ~8.4s of a reveal; absent entirely on an insecure origin (ResultBanner.svelte:29, 73); confirmation 1500ms.
*Fix:* once a sentence exists, Copy takes `.primary` and "Roll everything" demotes to "Roll again". Keep Copy mounted-but-disabled during reveals. Render a select-all affordance when `canCopy` is false.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable bolder`

## Persona Red Flags

**Power user (twenty re-rolls):** 2m48s of waiting. The escape hatch is a 0.9rem `--muted` checkbox shoved right by `margin-inline-start: auto` — lowest salience in the banner guarding the biggest time cost. `rollAll` clears all five facets with no history. No keyboard shortcut to roll. Five Roll targets at five y-positions 200-250px apart.

**First-timer:** nothing demonstrates output before they act; the placeholder is `--muted`/400, styled to recede. Three of five cards unarmed by default with no explanation. "How many / 1-4" invites the setting that most damages output. The zero-facet dead end is what curiosity produces in 30 seconds.

**Keyboard / screen-reader user:** five buttons named only "Roll" (FacetCard.svelte:99-101) — fix with `aria-label="Roll {facet.label}"`. Tab order (18 stops) puts the theme radio group before "Roll everything". The clamp rewrite is silent. `role=status` empties and refills 3-4× per roll, announcing an empty string and partial sentences — same root cause as P1, since `announcement` (ResultBanner.svelte:51-57) gates on `revealing`. Credit: focus correctly moved to the checkbox on card collapse (FacetCard.svelte:45-47).

## Minor Observations

- **Light `--muted` #5b6370 on `--bg` #f3f1ec measures 3.46:1 — fails WCAG AA.** Hits `.subtitle` (16px) and footer (13.6px). Same token on white cards passes at 6.06; dark passes at 8.22. Contradicts PRODUCT.md's stated AA bar.
- Ghost chips fixed at `width: 7.5rem` with ellipsis (FacetCard.svelte:301) truncate to "Roguel…" exactly when the tick curve slows so decoys can be read.
- The 3.9em mobile reserve applies to the placeholder — a ~90px hole on first paint. `reservedRows` already does the suppress-until-rolled trick.
- `button:hover` puts the global accent on the five per-facet Roll buttons; a hue-tinted border would tighten the system.
- Two "live" edges fire at once during a reveal: banner → accent, card → facet hue.
- Players (`{min:1,max:1}`) has no count row — a differently shaped card in a uniform stack, unexplained.
- Copy success and failure share one 1500ms timer; failure should persist.
- Animating `padding` on `.card` is the mechanism, not the contract — `grid-template-rows` or a transform collapse gets the same behavior off the layout thread.
- `scrapedAt` via `Intl.DateTimeFormat` inside a real `<time datetime>` (App.svelte:105-108). Small and correct.

## Questions to Consider

- If success is "copy and leave", why is Copy the third-loudest thing in the banner?
- Why can a user lose an idea they liked? A three-item "recent ideas" strip turns a slot machine into a shortlist tool.
- The plot chip is the only element that teaches. Why is that treatment reserved for one of five facets?
- Is `default: 2` genres a feature or a bug?
- Why is the tray vertical? What if the compartments were a grid, with the sentence inside it?
- Should the reveal be per-slot after the first roll?
- No URL state, no seed — two jammers can't look at the same sentence. Facet items already carry stable ids.
